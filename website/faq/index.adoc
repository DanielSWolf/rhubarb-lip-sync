= FAQ
:eleventy-layout: page

Here are answers to some frequently asked questions about Rhubarb Lip Sync.

== Can I use Rhubarb Lip Sync for real-time mouth animation?

No, that's currently not possible.

Rhubarb Lip Sync analyzes entire utterances—short segments of speech, usually a few seconds long.
It needs to process the full audio input before generating animation data.

In live settings, this kind of delayed processing isn't feasible.
Even if Rhubarb were rewritten to run continuously, the result would often appear *too late* to look convincing.

That's because spoken language involves anticipation.
Your mouth starts forming the shape for the next sound *before* the sound is audible.
For example, in the word *glue*, your lips form the *oo* shape while you're still saying the *g*.

Since a real-time system can't know what's coming next, it would always lag behind.
In practice, even a small delay—like 100 milliseconds—can make a mouth animation look out of sync.

If you're working with live or TTS-generated speech, your best option may be to use phoneme timing data provided directly by your TTS system.
In theory, Rhubarb could be modified to consume this data instead of analyzing audio, but that would require non-trivial development work.

== The mouth animation is jerky or imprecise. How can I get better results?

A few things can help improve the quality of the lip sync:

* Use a clean, high-quality voice recording.
  Avoid background noise, distortion, music, or heavy reverb.
* Include a transcript of the spoken dialog using the `-d` option.
  This helps Rhubarb recognize difficult or uncommon words—especially in English.
* Make sure the audio is dry and unfiltered.
  Audio effects may reduce clarity and confuse the recognizer.

That said, Rhubarb Lip Sync uses somewhat outdated speech recognition technology.
While it can produce solid results for many types of dialog, it's not perfect.

That's one reason why I'm currently working on *Rhubarb Lip Sync 2*, a full rewrite with improved recognition and animation quality.
